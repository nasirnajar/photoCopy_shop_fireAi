## 2. What I Observed

I visited Chenab Printers to understand how the shop handles customers during its regular working hours. I observed students coming in for printing, photocopying and stationery-related work.

During busy periods, multiple students sometimes arrive around the same time, particularly during class breaks and assignment submission periods. This can increase waiting times and make it difficult for students who have limited time between classes.

I wanted to understand whether recording customer visits at different times could help identify the shop's busiest hours.


## 3. Conversation with the Owner

I spoke with Mahafiz Qayoom, the owner of Chenab Printers, about the challenges he faces while managing the shop.

**Things discussed:**

1. He mentioned that things often get messy during assignment submission periods and examination time because many students visit the shop around the same time.
2. He said that knowing in advance how many students are likely to visit would help him prepare for the workload and manage the shop better.
3. He suggested the idea of having an app where students could enter their assignment deadlines, allowing him to get an idea of upcoming demand and expected customer flow.

**Something that surprised me:**

I initially thought the main problem was simply long queues. However, the conversation made me realise that the owner would find it even more useful to know about upcoming busy periods in advance, rather than only seeing how busy the shop is at a particular moment.
